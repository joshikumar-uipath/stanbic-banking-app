Presenter and admin

Whoever runs the demo · Launcher and the tools behind the scenes

The launcher opens every surface; the tools behind it are the bank’s systems stood in for — the case in Maestro, the Kafka stream, Finacle, the notification service and FaceID — plus the admin console that sets the brand, the market and the operating rules.

Open Launcher and the tools behind the scenes

Before you start

  • Open localhost:5180. Every card opens in a new tab.
  • Case management’s “▶ Run in Maestro” and “Approve {Stage}” act on the real tenant; the admin console’s Connect, Provision and “Publish to Data Fabric” write to the tenant you connect.

01

Open the demo and reset it

1 minSample · —3 steps

Open it

Role first: you pick who you are, and it opens the tool that role uses.

  1. 1The launcher

    Open localhost:5180.

    You should seeThe feature guide banner, then Who are you? — Relationship Manager, Operations analyst, Customer — each with its other surfaces and a “Step-by-step guide” link.

  2. 2Behind the scenes

    Scroll down.

    You should seeThe feature guide, Mock Kafka console, Mock Finacle UI, Notification service, Control tower, Case management and FaceID.

  3. 3Reset before a run

    Click “↺ Reset demo data”, then “Yes, reset”.

    You should seeThe pipeline reseeds: exceptions come back and ages are current. Branding is kept. Records on the shared tenant are not touched.

04

FaceID: the console and the kiosk

2 minSample · —6 steps

Open it

The selfie check is a liveness and 1:1 match service. The console is where the bank reviews every check; the kiosk is the walk-up version.

  1. 1Sign in

    Open FaceID Verify console and click “Sign in with Stanbic IBTC”.

    You should see“Verify a real, live person. In seconds.” No credentials are asked for.

  2. 2Dashboard

    You land on the dashboard.

    You should seeEnrolled identities, verifications today, pass rate, spoofs blocked, recent verifications and what needs attention.

  3. 3Identities

    Click Identities.

    You should seeEvery enrolled identity (the opened accounts) with “Verify now”, “Update photo” and remove.

  4. 4Settings

    Click Settings.

    You should seeFace match similarity 80%, liveness confidence 70%, detection mode, region and security.

  5. 5The kiosk

    Open FaceID kiosk. For a result without a camera add ?demo=success or ?demo=mismatch before the #.

    You should see“Customer Verification Complete” with face match and liveness passed. A real scan uses the camera and AWS.

  6. 6A failed match

    Use ?demo=mismatch.

    You should see“Verification Failed — the submitted selfie does not sufficiently match the identity document on file.”

05

Configure the demo

2 minSample · admin / admin5 steps

Open it

Brand, market, SLAs and routing are configuration, not code — the same app can be shown to another bank or country.

  1. 1Sign in

    Open localhost:5180/#admin (it is deliberately not on the launcher) and sign in with admin / admin.

    You should see“Demo admin gate — sign in with admin / admin.”

  2. 2Choose a configuration

    Pick a saved configuration or create one.

    You should see“Choose a configuration” with Load preset, Rename, Delete and Create. “Connect a UiPath tenant instead” uses a PAT.

  3. 3Branding and market

    Load one. The Branding tab holds the app name, colours, logo and Country.

    You should seeChanging Country relocalises names, currency, phone and ID formats and the KYC authorities — and reseeds the store.

  4. 4Operations

    Click Operations.

    You should seeStraight-through target, exception SLA, retry backoff and queue routing (cause → queue → owner) — what the control tower measures against.

  5. 5Features

    Click Features.

    You should seeFeature flags and the bottom-navigation tabs. “Save configuration” keeps it in this browser; “Publish to Data Fabric” writes it to the tenant.

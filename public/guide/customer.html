Customer

Adaeze Okafor, the signed-in customer in the banking app · Customer banking app

The customer opens an account from inside the Stanbic IBTC app they already bank with: six plain steps with their BVN and NIN, or a photo of a filled form that the case reads for them. The account opens while the checks run; they finish KYC — documents, a selfie and a signature — from a link whenever suits them, and follow every step on their status screen.

Open Customer banking app

Before you start

  • Open the launcher and click “On web” on the Customer card (or go to localhost:5180/#customer-web). “Customer” itself opens the phone view.
  • There is no sign-in: the app opens as Adaeze Okafor.
  • Demo autofill is on: leave any field empty and press Continue, and it fills with sample values.
  • “Submit application” and uploading a form start a real Maestro case on the staging tenant. Do it when you mean to.

01

Look around the banking app

2 minSample · Adaeze Okafor6 steps

Open it

Account opening is not a separate portal. It sits inside the everyday app, next to balances, payments and investments, which is where a customer would look for it.

  1. 1Start on Home

    The top bar holds Home, Pay & transfer, Open an account, Wealth and More, with the bell and the assistant on the right.

    You should see“Good afternoon, Adaeze”, a total balance of NGN 915,900.50, quick actions (Send, Pay, Top up, Scan) and her accounts and card.

  2. 2Open Pay & transfer

    Click “Pay & transfer”.

    You should seeNine payment types (Send Money, NIP Transfer, Scan & Pay, Pay Bill…) and recent recipients.

  3. 3Send money

    Click “Send Money”, enter a phone number and an amount, and click Continue.

    You should seeA review with the fee and total, and the line “This is a demo — no real money moves.”

  4. 4Confirm

    Click “Confirm & Send”.

    You should see“NGN 25,000.00 sent” with a reference, and Share, Copy ref and Receipt.

  5. 5Wealth and More

    Click Wealth for funds, protection and pension; click More for settings and the Apply section.

    You should seeInvest / Protect / Retire / Money in/out tabs and the Stanbic IBTC funds with their returns.

  6. 6Settings and services

    On More, Quick Access and App & Security hold the everyday settings.

    You should seeUnder Apply, “Open Account” is a placeholder; the real journey is the “Open an account” tab.

02

Open an account in six steps

3 minSample · New application8 steps

Open it

An account in about five minutes, starting from the two numbers every Nigerian customer has — BVN and NIN. Verification runs in the background, so the customer never waits on a queue.

  1. 1Open the tab

    Click “Open an account” in the top bar.

    You should see“A bank account in about five minutes.” and three promises: open in about five minutes, verified with NIMC & NIBSS, finish KYC whenever.

  2. 2Step 1 · About you

    Click “Open an account”. Enter your name exactly as on your NIN, date of birth, gender, marital status, mother’s maiden name, nationality, phone and email — or leave them for autofill.

    You should see“Let’s start with you”, Step 1 of 6, and the upload box “Have a completed form? Upload it” at the top.

  3. 3Step 2 · Your identity

    Click Continue. BVN, NIN, means of identification, ID number and expiry, and TIN (optional for a personal account).

    You should see“We verify these with NIBSS and NIMC in the background. You won’t wait for it.” If the details match an existing customer, a note says so.

  4. 4Step 3 · Where do you live?

    Click Continue. Address, city, state, LGA, years at the address, alternate phone.

    You should see“You can send proof of address later — it won’t hold up your account.”

  5. 5Step 4 · What do you do?

    Click Continue. Employment status, occupation, employer, annual income and source of funds.

    You should see“This sets the limits on your account, and we are required to ask.”

  6. 6Step 5 · Your new account

    Click Continue. Account type (Savings, Current or Domiciliary), purpose, preferred branch and next of kin.

    You should seeSavings · Everyday banking · Lagos main branch, and the next-of-kin block.

  7. 7Step 6 · Check your details

    Click Continue. Read the summary and tick the consent.

    You should seeName, phone, email, BVN, NIN, address, date of birth, identification, employment, account and next of kin in one list.

  8. 8Submit

    Click “Submit application”. In live use this starts the OnboardingCase in Maestro.

    You should see“Application received”, your reference (AO-2026-…) and “We’ll text you the moment your account number is ready”. Buttons: “Track my application” and “Finish KYC now”.

03

Upload a completed form instead of typing

2 minSample · fixtures/documents/adaeze-okafor/account-opening-form.pdf4 steps

Open it

Many customers already have a filled paper form. The case reads it with IXP while they are still on the screen, and they only check the fields the model was unsure of.

  1. 1Choose the form

    On step 1, click “Have a completed form? Upload it” and choose a PDF or photo of the account opening form. Sample forms are in the repo under fixtures/documents/.

    You should seeThe upload starts the case straight away, so extraction runs while you wait.

  2. 2Watch it read

    The review takes over the screen: “Extraction review · Reading the form”, the document on the right.

    You should see“The case is running the extraction. You can close this and come back — it keeps going.” “Confirm and continue” stays disabled until the case has parked the review task.

  3. 3Check what was read

    On a live case the fields arrive in about 30 seconds. In these screenshots the upload was sandboxed, so after three minutes the screen shows its own labelled sample.

    You should see“Showing a sample read — the live extraction was not available. Values below are illustrative.” Unsure fields sit at the top under “2 fields need checking”, each with its confidence.

  4. 4Correct and confirm

    Scroll the field groups, correct anything wrong, then click “Confirm and continue”. In live use this completes the Action Center task and the case moves on; the wizard fills in from what you confirmed.

    You should seeEvery field shows its confidence (96%, 87%…); blank boxes on the form are listed separately.

04

Track the application

1 minSample · The application you just submitted3 steps

Open it

The customer can see where the application is and which checks have passed, without phoning the branch.

  1. 1Open the status

    Click “Track my application” on the received screen.

    You should see“Your application”, the reference, a stage chip and the five progress steps: Application received → Verifying your identity → Identity verified → Account created → Fully activated.

  2. 2Read the checks

    Scroll to Checks and Activity.

    You should seeNIN – NIMC, BVN – NIBSS, AML, PEP and fraud screening and the selfie check, each Pending, Running or Passed. A live case reads its state from Maestro every 4 seconds; an unrun one plays the demo story.

  3. 3Come back later

    Next time, open “Open an account”: “Applications in progress” lists the applications made on this device. Tap one to reopen its status.

    You should seeAdaeze Okafor · AO-2026-… with its current state.

05

Finish KYC from the link

2 minSample · The newest case waiting on KYC (e.g. AO-2026-000104)5 steps

Open it

The account opens first and works with limits; KYC is three quick things the customer does from an SMS link when it suits them — no branch visit.

  1. 1Open the link

    Open localhost:5180/#kyc/DEMO01 (the launcher’s “KYC link”). The link opens the newest application waiting on documents or a selfie.

    You should see“Hello Adaeze 👋 Your account … is already open.” Three things: your documents, a quick selfie, your signature. “Takes about two minutes.”

  2. 2Your documents

    Click “Get started”. Tap “Means of identification” and “Proof of address”.

    You should seeEach tile turns green: “Uploaded · tap to replace”. (The tiles are a stand-in; no file picker opens.)

  3. 3A quick selfie

    Click Continue. The FaceID check opens in the page and asks for the camera.

    You should see“Let’s verify it’s you” and “Stay in Position”. This is the real FaceID liveness service on the staging host; finish the check, then click “I’ve finished the check”.

  4. 4Your signature

    Sign in the box with a finger or the mouse.

    You should see“Sign here … This is the signature we’ll hold on file.” “Clear” starts again.

  5. 5Done

    Click Finish.

    You should see“You’re all set. Your account is fully activated.” with the account number. On a live case, “fully activated” waits for Maestro’s Closure stage.

Ask the assistant, read notifications

1 minSample · Adaeze Okafor3 steps

Open it

Everyday questions answered in the app rather than by the contact centre.

  1. 1Open the assistant

    Click the chat icon in the top bar (“Ask Digibot”).

    You should see“Hi Adaeze 👋 I’m your Stanbic IBTC assistant” and chips: Check my balance, Send money, Pay a bill, Replace my card, Reset card PIN, Talk to support.

  2. 2Ask something

    Click “Check my balance”.

    You should seeThe balances of every account. The assistant is scripted in the POC; it does not call a model.

  3. 3Notifications

    Click the bell.

    You should seeBanking and Tips & Deals tabs with unread counts: statement ready, salary received, card payment due.

1 minSample · Adaeze Okafor3 steps

Open it

The customer app is phone-first; the web shell is the same screens on a laptop.

  1. 1Open the phone view

    From the launcher click the Customer card itself (localhost:5180/#customer-mobile).

    You should seeA phone frame with the header, balance and a bottom bar: Home, Pay, the raised “+” Open, Wealth, More.

  2. 2Open an account

    Tap the raised “+”.

    You should seeThe same “A bank account in about five minutes” screen, with “Applications in progress” underneath.

  3. 3A call from the voice agent

    When the voice agent service calls the customer about their application, a “UiBank is calling you” card appears on the phone with “This is me” and “Not me”. It only appears when the voice service sends a call, so it is not in these screenshots.

    You should seeThe card only shows on the phone view.

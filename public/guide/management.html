Management

Head of retail onboarding, operations leads · Control tower

The control tower is a six-page report in the Power BI look over the same case store: how many applications arrived, how many went straight through, where every application sits, why work stops, which channels perform and how long an account takes to open.

Open Control tower

Before you start

  • Open the launcher and click “Control tower” under Behind the scenes (localhost:5180/#tower).
  • It reads the local store plus a synthetic 60-day history; the Power BI chrome (File, Export, Share…) is for the look and is disabled.
  • “Simulate an application” creates a new record that is mirrored to Data Fabric. Leave it alone unless you want a moving number.

01

Read the report

3 minSample · Last 48 hours6 steps

Open it

One place to answer “how are we doing” with the same numbers operations works from.

  1. 1Overview

    The report opens on Overview.

    You should seeApplications, straight-through, accounts opened and exceptions against the prior period, applications received, the straight-through gauge against 80%, where every application sits and how work arrives.

  2. 2Exceptions & SLA

    Click “Exceptions & SLA” at the bottom.

    You should seeOpen work, breached SLA, at risk, oldest item, “Why work stops”, queue load by routing rule and aging.

  3. 3Flow

    Click Flow.

    You should seeThe account opening Sankey from channel through verification to outcome.

  4. 4Channels

    Click Channels.

    You should seeBulk payroll, branch and digital, intake and straight-through by channel, and top employers.

  5. 5Verification

    Click Verification.

    You should seeFirst-time right, failures, timeouts and retries, and result by check.

  6. 6Timing

    Click Timing.

    You should seeMedian and 90th percentile time to open, and when applications arrive by hour and weekday.

02

Filter and change the range

1 minSample · Bulk payroll3 steps

Open it

Every visual answers the same question for a slice: one channel, one outcome, one failing check.

  1. 1Open Filters

    Click Filters.

    You should seeChannel, verification, outcome and failing check, each with counts.

  2. 2Filter a channel

    Tick “Bulk payroll”.

    You should see“Filtered by: Channel: Bulk payroll” and every visual recalculated. “Clear all” removes it. Clicking a bar or a Sankey node filters the same way.

  3. 3Change the range

    Click “7 days”.

    You should seeThe same report over 7 days; 24 hours, 48 hours and 30 days are there too.

Relationship manager

A relationship manager on the branch desk · Employee desk

The RM’s workspace: a desk that ranks what needs them, a directory of every application however it arrived, a case view that says the next best action and where the case really is in Maestro, assisted capture for walk-ins and companies, bulk submissions for payroll drives, approvals within their authority and their book of clients — with the UiPath agents one click away.

Open Employee desk

Before you start

  • Open the launcher and click “On web” on the Relationship Manager card (localhost:5180/#employee-web).
  • You are signed in automatically with the demo token. If you sign out, sign back in with the demo login demo / demo.
  • The desk shows every record on the shared tenant, so you will see other people’s test applications too.
  • Buttons that act on the real tenant: “Submit application”, uploading a form, “▶ Run this case in Maestro”, “Approve … & advance”, “Confirm and continue” and Batches’ “Process N applications”. Voice agent panels are the real voice service — do not press its call controls.

01

Start the day on My desk

1 minSample · Blessing Eze (AO-2026-000107) at the top2 steps

Open it

An RM should not have to hunt for work. The desk ranks everything that needs them and shows each case’s live Maestro stage, so they can tell a customer where it is.

  1. 1Your next move

    The card at the top is the single most urgent item, with “Open AO-2026-…” and “Skip for now →”.

    You should see“Your next move · 1 of 62”, why it needs you, and the stat tiles: Needs you, Breaching SLA, In flight, Straight through.

  2. 2Work the lanes

    Scroll to “My desk”. Filter by All / Needs attention / In flight / Done, by source (Bulk / Single) and by type (Corporate / Individual).

    You should see“01 Needs you now” with Who · What · Maestro stage · Status · When. Rows marked LIVE are real Maestro instances. Click a row to open the case.

07

Capture an application with a customer

3 minSample · New application4 steps

Open it

Walk-ins and phone customers get the same guided capture as self-service, and the RM can open a company account too.

  1. 1Start

    Click “New application” (My desk or Open an account).

    You should seeThe capture overlay with “Individual | Corporate”, “Customer details” and the upload box. The six individual steps are the customer’s (see the customer guide).

  2. 2A company instead

    Click Corporate.

    You should see“Business details”: company name, RC number, TIN, category, sector, turnover, address, email and phone. Continue stays disabled until there is a company name.

  3. 3Directors & signatories

    Enter a company name and click Continue.

    You should see“Everyone who signs on the account. Each is verified with NIBSS in the background.” and “Add another director”.

  4. 4Check the details

    Click Continue.

    You should seeThe company and director summary and the signatories’ consent. “Submit application” starts the corporate case in Maestro; press Esc to leave without submitting.

08

Submit a bulk batch (AOP bundle)

4 minSample · “Load a sample bundle”8 steps

Open it

Business Name and Sole Trader drives arrive as piles of filled forms. One submission checks them all, rejects what is incomplete with a reason, and processes the rest in parallel.

  1. 1New submission

    Click Batches in the rail (or “Bulk upload” in Open an account).

    You should seeTwo channels: “Excel manifest” (structured at intake, no review needed) and “AOP bundle” (OCR extraction, ops review before creation).

  2. 2Load the bundle

    Choose “AOP bundle”, click “Load a sample bundle” (or “Choose .zip”), set the batch name, segment and branch.

    You should seeOne row per customer with the documents found and Complete or “Missing …”.

  3. 3Look at a document

    Click View on a row.

    You should seeThe document reader with a tab per document. For the sample bundle it says so: “Sample bundle — no file behind this entry.” A real .zip shows the real pages.

  4. 4Validate

    Click “Validate customers →”.

    You should see“Intake validation”: 9 submitted, 9 identified, 6 accepted, 3 rejected at intake — each with the reason (utility bill too old, no proof of address, no ID).

  5. 5Process

    Click “Process 6 applications →”. In live use the first 8 rows each start a real OnboardingCase.

    You should seeThe batch monitor: Verifying, Awaiting review, With authoriser, Exceptions, Auto-rejected, Accounts open, and the verification services (TIN, CAC, ID document, sanctions, watchlist, adverse media). These screenshots ran without live orchestration, which the monitor says.

  6. 6One customer

    Click a customer’s name.

    You should seeThe customer sheet: Case (the same case view as the desk), Documents and Extraction, with Prev / Next.

  7. 7What was read

    Click Extraction.

    You should see“Every source agrees”, each field with its confidence, and the verifications.

  8. 8The batch completes

    After the maker and verifier have done their part (see the bulk operations guide), open Batch again.

    You should see5 accounts open with their numbers, 1 parked as an exception.

Invite a payroll list (Excel manifest)

2 minSample · “Load a sample manifest”3 steps

Open it

For an employer drive the manifest is the control list: only the people the organisation named are onboarded, and each fills the form themselves.

  1. 1Load the manifest

    In New submission choose “Excel manifest” and click “Load a sample manifest” (or “Upload .csv”).

    You should seeReference, first and last name, BVN, NIN, phone and email for 12 people.

  2. 2Validate

    Click “Validate 12 rows →”.

    You should see12 submitted, 12 accepted, “Every row passed intake. Nothing to send back.”

  3. 3Watch who finishes

    Click “Process 12 applications →”, then Invitations.

    You should seeInvited 12 · Opened the link · Completed · No response, the funnel, and each person with where they got to. “Re-send the link to all” and “Send by WhatsApp” nudge the rest (nothing is sent in the POC).

Bulk operations

Operations maker and verifier on a bulk batch · Employee desk → Batches

For a bundle of scanned forms the BRD makes a person look before an account is created. The maker compares what was read with the form and approves, corrects or flags it; a different person, the verifier, then authorises the accounts, with PND by rule. Anything that cannot be reconciled becomes a managed exception with the full evidence pack.

Open Employee desk → Batches

Before you start

  • Submit a bundle first (Relationship manager → “Submit a bulk batch”), so there is something to review.
  • Open Batches and use “Acting as” at the top right: Relationship Manager · Maker · Verifier. Each persona only sees its own screens — §9.1 keeps maker and checker apart.
  • Everything on these screens is local to the browser, apart from the Maestro cases the first 8 rows start when processed.

01

Maker: review what was read off the form

3 minSample · The AOP batch you submitted6 steps

Open it

OCR is not 100% accurate, so each OCR-read application passes a brief human checkpoint (BR-DOC-10). The maker sees the form, the read, the reconciliation and the verifications together.

  1. 1Switch to Maker

    In Batches click Maker under “Acting as”.

    You should see“Extraction review · 5 waiting”, lowest confidence first, with lenses All / Low confidence / Reconciliation / Aged over 24h and “Review next →”.

  2. 2Open one

    Click Review on the first row.

    You should seeThe AOP on the left with unsure boxes highlighted, and on the right “What was read off the form” with a confidence per field.

  3. 3Reconciliation

    Look at the green banner.

    You should see“Every source agrees — name and date of birth match across the form, NIBSS and NIMC.” A disagreement is named here.

  4. 4Verification outcomes

    Scroll down.

    You should seeTIN – FIRS, CAC registry, ID document, sanctions, internal watchlist, adverse media, NIN and BVN — already run in parallel. Every field you change is logged with your name, the time and the value before.

  5. 5Or flag it

    Click “Flag as exception”.

    You should seeA bar at the bottom: “What is the concern? It travels with the case.” with “Raise the exception” (enabled once you type) and Cancel.

  6. 6Approve

    Click “Approve and take the next →” until the queue is empty.

    You should see“Queue is clear. Everything on this batch has been confirmed and is with the verifier.”

02

Verifier: authorise the accounts

1 minSample · The same batch2 steps

Open it

A different person creates the accounts. Business accounts open on PND by rule (§9.2), and the verifier can lift it only against a recorded deferral.

  1. 1Switch to Verifier

    Click Verifier under “Acting as”.

    You should see“Authorise account opening · 5 ready”, each with product and “PND on”, and on the right “Business accounts open on PND”, “Open operative instead — overrides the PND rule” with a deferral reference, and the maker trail.

  2. 2Create the accounts

    Click “Create 5 accounts →”.

    You should see“Nothing is waiting on you.” The accounts are created in the mock Finacle and the RM sees the numbers on the batch.

03

Requester: dispose of an exception

1 minSample · The parked row on the batch2 steps

Open it

An exception carries its full evidence pack, so whoever receives it does not repeat any verification — and cannot close it without a reason.

  1. 1Open the exception

    As Relationship Manager, open Batch and click “Parked →” on the red row (or the Exceptions tab).

    You should see“What triggered this” (here a verification service persistently unavailable), who it is routed to, and every check with its result, time and evidence.

  2. 2Decide

    Pick a “Reason code” and write a “Supporting note”, then “Resolve and resume the case”, “Send to Compliance” or “Return to the customer”.

    You should seeThe buttons stay disabled until both are filled: “A disposition without a reason and a note is not accepted — every decision has to be reconstructable.”

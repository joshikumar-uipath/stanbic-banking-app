Operations analyst

An operations analyst on exception triage · Operations Workbench

Operations sees every stuck case across the bank, grouped by why it stopped. They clear a cause in one action — retry every NIN check that hit the same gateway timeout — work single cases from the keyboard, and turn a decision they keep making by hand into a rule.

Open Operations Workbench

Before you start

  • Open the launcher and click the Operations analyst card (localhost:5180/#ops).
  • Actions on the seeded cases stay in the browser. In a case view, “▶ Run in Maestro” and “Approve … & advance” act on the real tenant.
  • Keyboard in a case: J / K next and previous, A accept, E escalate, X reject, Esc close.

01

Triage by cause, not by case

2 minSample · Needs attention5 steps

Open it

Most stuck cases share a handful of causes. Clearing the cause clears every case behind it at once.

  1. 1Needs attention

    The workbench opens on Needs attention.

    You should seeThe banner (“27 cases are past SLA… Clear the cause, not the cases”), straight-through, past SLA, unassigned and hours returned, and the “Pattern found” card.

  2. 2Root causes

    Scroll to Root causes.

    You should seeNIMC gateway timeout, BVN did not verify, liveness below threshold, NIN name mismatch, proof of address out of policy, bulk upload partly failed — each with a bulk action and “Review one by one”.

  3. 3Clear a cause

    Click “Retry NIN check on all (8)”.

    You should seeA confirmation: “Retry NIN check → 8 cases back in the flow”, and the counts drop.

  4. 4Or open a queue

    Click a pipeline step in the rail, e.g. Liveness.

    You should see“Pending liveness” with every case at that step.

  5. 5The case list

    On Needs attention, scroll to “Individual cases”; sort by Urgency, Risk, Age or Name.

    You should seeCustomer with reason and channel, the Maestro stage bar, outcome, SLA and assignee.

02

Resolve one case

2 minSample · Aisha Yusuf · AO-2026-000007 (name mismatch)5 steps

Open it

When a case does need a person, everything they need is on one screen and the decision is one key.

  1. 1Open a name mismatch

    Click Aisha Yusuf.

    You should seeThe problem (“NIMC holds a different spelling”), “Aisha Yusuff” on the form against “Aisha Yusuf” at NIMC, the agent trail, the checks with the risk score, and the applicant.

  2. 2A liveness failure

    Open Blessing Okoro.

    You should seeThe liveness gauge at 0.41 against 0.60, and “Request a new selfie” / “Approve manually”.

  3. 3A live case

    Open Blessing Eze.

    You should see“Live in Maestro · Running” with the stage chips and the case’s real stage. “Approve … & advance” here completes the real task.

  4. 4Accept

    On Aisha Yusuf click “Accept NIMC name and continue” (or press A).

    You should seeA confirmation for AO-2026-000007 and the next case opens.

  5. 5A bulk failure

    Click “Bulk failures”, then Ashaka Cement payroll.

    You should see“12 of 180 rows failed at the same step”, with “Retry the 12 failed rows” and “Download the failures”.

03

Turn a pattern into a rule

2 minSample · Pattern found: 47 name-mismatch decisions4 steps

Open it

If people accept the same thing 47 times out of 47, it is a policy nobody wrote down. Promoting it returns hours every week.

  1. 1Open Rules

    Click Rules in the rail.

    You should seeThe candidate at the top and the live rules, each with how many cases it has handled and a Pause button.

  2. 2Review the evidence

    Click “Review the 47 decisions”.

    You should see“Every name mismatch decision, 12 May to 14 July”: the histogram, a threshold slider and every decision.

  3. 3Promote

    Click “Promote at 0.91”.

    You should see“Rule live at 0.91. 2 open cases cleared.” It joins the live rules.

  4. 4The decision table

    Click Edit.

    You should seeThe OnboardingAutomation DMN table — check, condition, automated action — with “Download .dmn”.
